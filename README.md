Features :

1. Multi-language Translation: Students can translate each FAQ answer directly within the FAQ card into multiple languages such as English, Hindi, Punjabi, and Spanish.

2. Bookmark FAQs: Students can save important FAQs directly from the FAQ card to their personal bookmarks for quick access later.

3. Ask Yaksha AI: Students can click the "Ask AI About This" button inside any FAQ to ask follow-up questions and get instant clarification related to that specific topic.

5. Category-based Navigation: Students can browse FAQs by categories such as About Internship, NOC, Certificates, and Projects for faster information access.

6. Saved FAQs Section: Students can view all their bookmarked FAQs in the "My Saved FAQs" section for quick future reference.

7. Smart Sorting: Students can switch between Index and Popular views to find FAQs either by category or based on popularity.

8. FAQ Count Indicators: Each category displays the total number of available FAQs, helping students quickly identify relevant topics.

9. Community Discussion Forum: Students can post their questions directly from the FAQ portal if they cannot find the required information.

10. Thread-based Queries: Each question creates a discussion thread where coordinators and other students can respond and share guidance.

11. Verified Answers: Coordinators can review useful discussions and mark verified responses as official FAQs for future reference.

12. Quick Revisit: The portal automatically keeps track of previously viewed FAQs, making it easier for students to continue where they left off.

13. Yaksha AI Assistant: Students can interact with the built-in Yaksha AI chatbot to get instant answers, personalized guidance, and support for internship-related queries such as NOC validation, certificates, stipends, team formation, and Rosetta Journal requirements.

14. Yaksha Voice Assistant: Students can interact with Yaksha AI using voice commands to ask internship-related questions and receive instant spoken responses, making information access faster and more convenient.

15. Community FAQ Contributions: Students can contribute answers to existing FAQs by submitting responses for admin review and moderation, helping build a collaborative and continuously improving knowledge base.

16. Interactive FAQ Hub: Students can browse categorized FAQs, view detailed answers, and contribute community responses directly from the FAQ cards to enhance the shared knowledge base.

17. Support Ticket System: Students can raise support tickets by selecting a category, setting priority levels, and providing issue details, enabling the admin team to track, manage, and resolve concerns efficiently through a unique ticket ID.

18. Categorized FAQ System: All FAQs are organized into clear categories such as Internship, NOC, Rosetta Journal, Stipends, and Certificates, helping students quickly find relevant information, save time, and avoid confusion.
